Valve marks unreachable roofs/ledges like the fix does (it leaves about half spawnable: small ledges beside the
route). Valve navs: no JUMP areas, few CROUCH, median area 1875 u2 (ours 1250-1875). Mid-campaign Valve maps have no

1. HIGH: Nav Source = "Made by the game" never gets the unreachable marks (mark_unreachable only runs in
   navpredict._finish). Fix: after the game's nav_save, load the .nav, mark, write back (like clear_nav_analysis), or
   do the walk in the navmark Squirrel script.
2. HIGH: Zombie Climbs / Zombies Climb Walls linked the street up onto roofs before the reachability walk, undoing
   the marks. **Fixed (a267cc1): reachability is worked out before the climb links.**
3. MEDIUM: the auto marks look like the user's own EMPTY boxes in the nav view (navview.py SPAWN colours); record
   auto-marked ids and show them distinctly; navanalysis.analyse calls drop-down roofs "not islands" while the
   marks treat them as unreachable (document).
4. MEDIUM: no start room, or a Start Safe Room box catching no nav areas: nothing is marked, silently. Fix: seed
   from areas under info_player_start / info_survivor_position; warn when a PLAYER_START region marks 0 areas.
5. LOW: decompiled Valve maps have no PLAYER_START: seed from CHECKPOINT areas holding the survivor spawns.
6. LOW, correct: Control Volume cuts (no area straddles the boundary). With Nav Source = Game the volumes do
   nothing: needs a UI note. Full-map nav with 0-1 commons: still unexplained, measure in game.
7. Checked right: the walk follows one-way drops (everything past a drop on the route stays spawnable) and ladders
   both ways; marks survive the worker pickling, write_generated_nav, navanalyze and cached(); spawn_block_problems
   runs before the marks; safe-room regions aren't padded upward.
