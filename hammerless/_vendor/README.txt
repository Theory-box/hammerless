manifold3d 3.5.4 (Apache-2.0, https://github.com/elalish/manifold): the geometry kernel of Convert to
Brushes (Extend-or-Fill). Two builds, Windows x64, from the PyPI wheels, unchanged: cp311 for Python 3.11 (Blender 4.2-4.5) and cp313 for
Python 3.13 (Blender 5.2; sha256 of its wheel 76975aa4ebd1...1553cf4). Python loads the one that fits. They share the msvcp140 DLL.
